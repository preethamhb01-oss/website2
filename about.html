ABOUT ME

I was born on 15/05/2008 in Chikkamagaluru, Karnataka, and when i turn back my journey has always been a combination of academics, cricket, and curiosity about technology.

I completed my SSLC at MDRS Bidarahalli, scoring 529/625, and continued my PUC education at St. Joseph's PU College, Chikkamagaluru, where I scored 562/600.

Cricket has been a major part of my life. 🏏 I have participated in local tournaments, winning 7 local tournaments, and one of my proudest moments was leading my team as captain and winning an inter-class cricket match. I enjoy the responsibility of captaincy and the teamwork, discipline, and decision-making that cricket teaches me. My favourite cricketer is Pat Cummins.

After PUC, I started my engineering journey, where my interest in technology became much stronger. I began learning Python, C programming, HTML, CSS, Git & GitHub, and DSA, while also building my own projects.

I believe in learning by doing. Instead of only studying concepts, I try to turn what I learn into projects such as a Bank Simulator , Grocery Store, Cricket projects, and websites.

Today, I'm a first-year engineering student working toward becoming a skilled software developer. My journey is still at the beginning, and I'm continuously learning, building, improving, and exploring new technologies.

From the cricket ground to the coding screen — I'm building my own journey, one step at a time. 🚀
